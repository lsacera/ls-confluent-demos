- EMT Bus Telemetry (11 buses on 7 routes):
  • Lines: 1, 3, 6, 27, 74, 146, N21 (night service)

  • Passenger occupancy (sensors detect crowding)
  • Delay tracking vs scheduled times

- Citizen Service Requests (Avisos Madrid - línea 010):
  • Categories: Street lighting, cleaning, potholes, parks, urban furniture
  • Priority levels: URGENTE (4h SLA), ALTA (24h), MEDIA (72h), BAJA (7 days)
  • Workflow: Open → In Progress → Validation → Resolved → Closed
  • Geolocation for heat map analysis

DATA STREAMING ARCHITECTURE:
---------------------------
- All sensors publish to Kafka topics in Avro format
- Schema Registry enforces data contracts (prevents bad data)
- 4 primary topics:
  • smartcity-traffic (6 partitions, 7-day retention)
  • smartcity-airquality (3 partitions, 7-day retention)
  • smartcity-emtbus (3 partitions, 7-day retention)
  • smartcity-service (3 partitions, 30-day retention)

APACHE FLINK PROCESSING:
------------------------
Seven real-time SQL queries processing urban data continuously:

1. Traffic Stream: Clean sensor data with watermarks for late arrivals
2. Air Quality Stream: Structured pollution measurements
3. Traffic Congestion Alerts: Real-time detection (CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM)
   - CRITICAL: Speed < 5 km/h + occupancy > 95% (blocked)
   - HIGH: Speed < 15 km/h + occupancy > 85% (heavy congestion)
   - MEDIUM: Speed < 30 km/h + occupancy > 70% (moderate congestion)

4. District Aggregations: 5-minute windows combining traffic + air quality
   - Which districts have worst traffic AND worst air?
   - Correlation analysis for policy decisions

5. EMT Bus Performance: 5-minute windows per bus line
   - Average delays, on-time percentage, overcrowding detection
   - Route optimization recommendations

6. Citizen Services SLA: 1-hour windows tracking ticket resolution
   - SLA compliance by category and priority
   - Overdue ticket alerts for operations teams

7. City Health Score: 10-minute windows with weighted scoring
   - Traffic Fluidity: 30% (speed-based metric)
   - Air Quality: 30% (AQI-based metric)
   - EMT Reliability: 20% (delay-based metric)
   - Citizen Services: 20% (resolution rate)
   - Overall score: 0-100 with status (EXCELLENT/GOOD/MODERATE/POOR)

All queries use:
- TIMESTAMP_LTZ(3) for millisecond precision
- Watermarks for out-of-order event handling (10-second tolerance)
- Windowed aggregations (5-min, 10-min, 1-hour)
- State management with TTL for memory optimization"

POLLUTANT MEASUREMENTS:
-----------------------
□ NO2 (Nitrogen Dioxide): Traffic pollution indicator
  - Limit: 40 µg/m³ (EU annual average)
  - Bad: >100 µg/m³ (immediate health risk)

□ PM2.5 (Fine Particulate Matter): Respiratory health risk
  - Good: <12 µg/m³
  - Moderate: 12-35 µg/m³
  - Bad: >35 µg/m³ (increased mortality risk)

□ PM10 (Coarse Particulate Matter): Construction, road dust
  - Good: <20 µg/m³
  - Bad: >50 µg/m³

□ O3 (Ozone): Forms in sunlight from traffic emissions
  - Peaks 12-4 PM on sunny days
  - Health risk: respiratory irritation

□ CO (Carbon Monoxide): Vehicle emissions
  - Normal: 0.3-0.8 mg/m³
  - Dangerous: >3 mg/m³

"Real-time pollutant data enables:
- Health alerts (asthma patients avoid high-pollution areas)
